    Data & methodology

    These are monthly averages from Statistics Canada’s selected-products table, based on retail transactions. Product descriptions specify package sizes or price units. The series are published in Canadian dollars and don’t represent quotes from a particular retailer.

    Per kg or per litre divides the average by the published pack size, where it specifies a weight or volume. An index of 100 represents the first shared observed month in the selected area and date filters. A dash means the source lacked an observation. Price changes use months with published observations in all selected areas.
